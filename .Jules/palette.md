## 2026-04-09 - [Strategic celebration and precise filtering]
**Learning:** Moving celebration animations (st.balloons) to terminal success states (100% completion) provides much higher psychological reward than on-load triggers. Human-readable labels for boolean states (e.g., "Annotated" vs "True") significantly reduce cognitive load in data-heavy apps.
**Action:** Always trigger celebratory feedback only after specific user accomplishments. Prefer semantic labels over raw boolean values in selectboxes.
