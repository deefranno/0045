## 2025-05-14 - [Streamlit UX Polishing]
**Learning:** For task-oriented apps like annotation tools, gating celebratory effects like `st.balloons()` behind 100% progress provides a much stronger reward signal than showing them on initial load. Additionally, using `st.progress` alongside metrics creates a more cohesive sense of advancement.
**Action:** Always consider the "completion milestone" when adding animations and pair quantitative metrics with visual progress indicators.
