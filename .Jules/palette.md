## 2025-05-14 - [Streamlit 1.56.0 Layout Changes]
**Learning:** In Streamlit 1.56.0+, `use_container_width=True` is deprecated for `st.dataframe` and `st.data_editor`. The new standard is `width="stretch"`.
**Action:** Use `width="stretch"` for data elements to ensure full-width layout and future compatibility.
